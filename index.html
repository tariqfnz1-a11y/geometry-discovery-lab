📏 Line, Ray, or Segment?

Drag the green point B — or use the slider. The label updates as the shape changes.

Drag point B on the canvas

👆 Try dragging the green dot directly

Why it matters

Line extends forever both ways — no endpoints.

Segment has two fixed endpoints, A and B — that's what you're dragging.

Ray has one endpoint and shoots off forever in one direction.

💡 As you drag B, the segment AB gets longer or shorter — but a true "line" wouldn't change length, because it never ends!

The angle family

🟢 Acute — less than 90°, a tight little wedge

🟦 Right — exactly 90°, a perfect corner

🟧 Obtuse — 90°–180°, a wide-open wedge

🟥 Straight — exactly 180°, a flat line

🟪 Reflex — 180°–360°, the "long way around"

💡 Notice the colored wedge fills more of the circle as the angle grows — that fraction IS the angle.

▭ Rectangle: Perimeter vs Area

Toggle the grid to literally count the unit squares that make up the area.

Two different ideas

Perimeter = the fence around the outside → 2 × (L + W)

Area = the carpet that fills the inside → L × W

💡 Turn on the grid: every little square is 1 unit². Count the rows (W of them) times the columns (L of them) — that's exactly L × W.

🔵 Circle Explorer

See circumference "unroll" into a straight line, and area "unfold" into a near-rectangle.

Circle relationships

Diameter = 2 × radius

Circumference = 2πr — the distance if you "unrolled" the rim into a straight line

Area = πr² — cut the circle into thin pizza slices, alternate them, and they form a shape close to a rectangle with height r and base πr!

💡 Click "Unfold area" to see the pizza-slice trick that explains where πr² comes from.
