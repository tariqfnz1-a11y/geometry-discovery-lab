📘 Geometry Study Notes

Geometry is the branch of mathematics that studies shapes, sizes, positions, angles and space.

📐 Angles

  • Acute angle: less than 90°.
  • Right angle: exactly 90°.
  • Obtuse angle: greater than 90° but less than 180°.
  • Straight angle: exactly 180°.
  • Reflex angle: greater than 180° but less than 360°.

🔺 Triangles

  • Equilateral triangle — three equal sides.
  • Isosceles triangle — two equal sides.
  • Scalene triangle — all sides different.
  • Right triangle — contains one right angle.

⬛ Quadrilaterals

  • Square
  • Rectangle
  • Parallelogram
  • Rhombus
  • Trapezium

📏 Perimeter

Perimeter = total distance around a shape

🟦 Area

Rectangle:
Area = length × width

⭕ Circle

Diameter:
d = 2r

Circumference:
C = 2πr

Area:
A = πr²

📦 Volume

Cuboid:
Volume = length × width × height

💡 Real-Life Geometry Problems

Problem 1 — Garden

A rectangular garden is 12 m long and 8 m wide. Find its perimeter.

Perimeter = 2 × (12 + 8) = 40 m
Problem 2 — Floor

A rectangular floor is 10 m long and 6 m wide. Find its area.

Area = 10 × 6 = 60 m²
Problem 3 — Triangle

The angles of a triangle are 50°, 60° and 70°. What is the sum of the angles?

50 + 60 + 70 = 180°
Problem 4 — Circle

A circle has a radius of 7 cm. Using π ≈ 22/7, find its circumference.

C = 2πr

= 2 × 22/7 × 7

= 44 cm
