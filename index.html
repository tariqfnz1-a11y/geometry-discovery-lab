📚 Interactive Study Notes

📐 Angles

An angle is formed when two rays meet at a common endpoint.

The common endpoint is called the vertex.

🔺 Triangles

A triangle has three sides and three angles.

The interior angles of a triangle add up to 180°.

▭ Quadrilaterals

A quadrilateral has four sides.

The interior angles add up to 360°.

🔵 Circles

A circle is a curved closed shape.

Radius goes from the centre to the circumference.

Diameter passes through the centre and equals twice the radius.

📊 Area & Perimeter

Area measures the surface inside a shape.

Perimeter measures the distance around a shape.

🔄 Transformations

Translations move shapes.

Rotations turn shapes.

Reflections flip shapes.
