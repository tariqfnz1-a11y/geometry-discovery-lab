📚 Study Notes

📐 Angles
An angle is formed by two rays meeting at a common endpoint called the vertex.
🔺 Triangles
A triangle has three sides and three angles. The interior angles total 180°.
▭ Quadrilaterals
A quadrilateral has four sides. Its interior angles total 360°.
🔵 Circles
Radius is the distance from the centre to the circumference. Diameter is twice the radius.
📊 Area
Area measures the surface inside a two-dimensional shape.
🧊 Volume
Volume measures the amount of three-dimensional space occupied by an object.
🔄 Transformations
Translations move shapes, rotations turn shapes, and reflections flip shapes.
